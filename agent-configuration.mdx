## Voice Instructions

Tell your agent who it is and what it knows.

### What to include

**Who:** "You are the receptionist for [Business Name]"

**What you offer:** List services, prices, and policies

**How to handle requests:** "If someone wants to book, ask for their name and preferred time"

**What NOT to do:** "Do not give medical advice. Transfer to the doctor instead."

### Writing tips

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Be specific">
    Instead of "Answer questions about services," write: "We offer cleanings ($150), fillings ($200), and whitening ($500)."
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Include examples">
    "If someone asks about returns, say: We accept returns within 30 days with receipt."
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Set boundaries">
    "Do not discuss pricing with competitors. Do not promise delivery times."
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
The AI Refinement button can help improve your instructions. Tell it what to add: "Add our cancellation policy" or "Make it friendlier."
</Tip>

## Greeting

The first thing callers hear. Keep it short.

**Requirements:**
- 20-200 characters
- Under 15 seconds when spoken

**Examples:**

<CodeGroup>
```text Professional
"Thank you for calling Bright Smile Dental. This is Emma, your AI assistant. How can I help you?"
```

```text Friendly
"Hey there! You've reached Pete's Pizza. I'm your virtual assistant. What can I get started for you?"
```

```text Short
"Smith & Associates. How may I direct your call?"
```
</CodeGroup>

<Warning>
Long greetings make callers hang up. Keep it under 15 seconds.
</Warning>

## Advanced Settings

### Creativity (Temperature)

Controls how creative vs. factual your agent sounds.

| Level | Best for |
|-------|----------|
| 0.0 - 0.3 | Medical, legal, technical |
| 0.3 - 0.5 | Customer service, scheduling |
| 0.5 - 0.7 | General inquiries (default) |
| 0.7 - 0.9 | Sales, marketing |

<Tip>
Start with 0.6. Lower if answers feel wrong. Raise if responses seem robotic.
</Tip>

### Max Call Duration

End calls automatically after a set time.

- **Quick questions:** 2-3 minutes
- **Appointments:** 5-7 minutes
- **Support calls:** 10-15 minutes

<Warning>
When time runs out, the agent says: "I need to end this call now. Please call back if you need more help. Goodbye!"
</Warning>

## Testing your changes

1. Click **Test** on your agent page
2. Call your agent's number
3. Check:
   - Does the greeting sound right?
   - Does it know your business info?
   - Are responses helpful?

<Tip>
Make one change at a time. Test each change before moving to the next.
</Tip>

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Agent gives wrong information">
    Add specific facts to Voice Instructions. Upload a document to the knowledge base with correct info.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Agent is too chatty or too brief">
    Adjust creativity. Add: "Keep responses under 30 seconds" or "Provide detailed explanations."
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Calls end unexpectedly">
    Check if Max Call Duration is too short. Increase it in Advanced Settings.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

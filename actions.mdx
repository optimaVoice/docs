## Connecting an app

### Step 1: Find the app

1. Go to **Actions**
2. Browse or search for the app you want

### Step 2: Connect

1. Click **Connect**
2. Sign in to your account (OAuth)
3. Allow permissions

<Note>
We use OAuth — we never see your password. You're redirected to the official app login.
</Tip>

### Step 3: Enable actions

1. Go to your agent's settings
2. Click **Add Action**
3. Select the app
4. Choose which actions to enable

---

## Setting up actions in your agent

After connecting, tell your agent when to use each action.

Add to **Voice Instructions**:

```
When a customer wants to book an appointment:
1. Ask for their preferred date and time
2. Check Google Calendar for availability
3. If available, book the appointment
4. Confirm the booking to the customer
5. Send a confirmation email

When someone wants to speak to a person:
1. Transfer the call to 555-1234
```

<Tip>
Be explicit. Agents won't guess — they follow your instructions exactly.
</Tip>

## Common setups

### Appointment booking

1. Connect Google Calendar
2. Enable "Create Event"
3. Add instructions for scheduling

**What happens:**
- Customer: "I'd like to book Thursday at 2pm"
- Agent checks calendar → books slot → confirms to customer

### Email confirmations

1. Connect Gmail
2. Enable "Send Email"
3. Add instructions

**What happens:**
- After booking, agent sends confirmation email automatically

### Team notifications

1. Connect Slack
2. Enable "Send Message"
3. Add instructions

**What happens:**
- New lead? Agent notifies #sales channel
- Support request? Agent notifies #support

## Managing connections

### View connected apps

1. Go to **Actions**
2. Click **Connected** tab
3. See all active integrations

### Disconnect

1. Find the app
2. Click **Disconnect**
3. Confirm

<Warning>
Disconnecting stops agents from using that app immediately.
</Tip>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Connection fails">
    Check: Popups allowed? Using correct account? Try again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Agent doesn't perform action">
    Check: Action enabled in agent settings? Instructions mention when to use it?
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Action fails during call">
    Check: Connected account has permission? Data valid? Try reconnecting.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Rate limits

Each app has limits:
- Google Calendar: ~1,000 requests/day
- Gmail: ~500 emails/day
- Slack: ~1 message/second

<Note>
Most businesses never hit these limits. Contact support for enterprise increases.
</Tip>

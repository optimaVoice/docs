Purchase a number or use your existing one. Customers call the number, your agent answers.

---

## Purchase a number (Recommended)

### Step 1: Add a number

1. Go to **Phone Numbers**
2. Click **Add Number**
3. Select **Purchase a number**

### Step 2: Choose your country

Select where you want the number. Popular options:
- United States ($1-2/month)
- Canada ($2/month)
- United Kingdom ($2-5/month)

<Tip>
Local numbers build trust with local customers. Toll-free numbers (800 numbers) look professional for national businesses.
</Tip>

### Step 3: Find a number

- Browse available numbers
- Or search by area code for a local presence
- Click **Purchase** on your choice

### Step 4: Assign to agent

After purchase:
1. Select which agent handles calls to this number
2. Click **Assign**

Done! Your agent is now receiving calls.

---

## Use your existing number

Already have a business number? Forward it to Optima Voice.

1. Click **Add Number**
2. Select **Manual Entry**
3. Enter your phone number
4. We'll give you forwarding instructions to set up with your carrier

<Warning>
Manual setup requires some technical knowledge. Most users prefer purchasing a number for instant setup.
</Tip>

---

## Managing numbers

### Assign to a different agent

1. Find the number in your list
2. Click **Reassign**
3. Select the new agent

Reassignment takes effect immediately.

### Remove a number

1. Click **Delete** on the number
2. Confirm

<Warning>
Deleted purchased numbers return to the pool. You can't guarantee getting the same number back.
</Tip>

## Number status

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| ✅ Active | Assigned to agent, receiving calls |
| ⚠️ Unassigned | No agent assigned |
| 🔄 Provisioning | Being set up (wait 1-2 minutes) |

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Call doesn't connect">
    Check: Is the number assigned to an agent? Is the agent configured? Try again in 2 minutes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Audio quality is poor">
    Check your internet connection. Agents need at least 1 Mbps. Try calling from a different network.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Agent doesn't respond">
    Verify the agent has a voice selected and Voice Instructions are not empty.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

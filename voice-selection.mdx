## Finding your voice

### 1. Set your language

Choose the language your customers speak. We have voices for:
- English (US, UK, Australian, Indian)
- Spanish, French, German, Italian
- Portuguese, Dutch, Polish
- And 50+ more

### 2. Browse or search

Use filters to narrow down:
- Language
- Gender (Male, Female, All)
- Keywords: "professional," "friendly," "authoritative"

Or type a specific voice name.

### 3. Preview

Click the **play** button on any voice. You'll hear a sample phrase.

What to listen for:
- **Clarity** — Can you understand every word?
- **Tone** — Does it match your brand?
- **Pacing** — Too fast, too slow, or just right?

<Tip>
Preview 3-5 voices before deciding. Your first choice isn't always the best.
</Tip>

## Changing voices

1. Go to your agent's settings
2. Click **Voice**
3. Browse and preview
4. Click to select

Changes take effect immediately for new calls.

<Warning>
Active calls continue with the old voice. Only new calls use the updated voice.
</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I upload my own voice?">
    Not yet. We use professional AI voices for consistent quality. Custom voice cloning is coming for enterprise plans.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can different agents have different voices?">
    Yes. Each agent has its own voice. You can have a professional voice for reception and a friendly voice for sales.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if a word is mispronounced?">
    Add pronunciation hints in Voice Instructions: "When saying 'Optima,' pronounce it as 'Op-tih-mah.'"
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

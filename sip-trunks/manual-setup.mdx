## Overview

Already have a phone number with your current provider? You can connect it to Optima Voice so your AI agent handles all incoming calls — no need to buy a new number.

**How it works:** You tell your phone provider to send calls to Optima Voice. We handle the rest.

**Time to complete:** 5–10 minutes

---

## What You Need

Before you start:

- A phone number with an existing provider (Telnyx, Twilio, Vonage, Zadarma, or any VoIP provider)
- Access to your provider's settings panel
- An Optima Voice account with at least one agent set up

---

## Step 1: Add Your Number in Optima Voice

1. Log in to [Optima Voice](https://app.optima-voice.com)
2. Click **Phone Numbers** in the left menu
3. Click the **Add Number** button
4. Select the **Manual Configuration** tab
5. Fill in the details:
   - **Trunk Name** — A label for this number (e.g. "Main Reception Line")
   - **Phone Number** — Your existing number, starting with + and country code (e.g. `+12025551234`)
   - **Assigned Agent** — Choose which agent should answer calls to this number
6. Click **Create Phone Number**

<Note>
You can change the assigned agent at any time from the Phone Numbers page.
</Note>

---

## Step 2: Point Your Provider to Optima Voice

In your phone provider's settings, find where you configure **call forwarding** or **SIP destination** and set the destination to:

```
sip:sip.optima-voice.com:5060
```

This tells your provider to send incoming calls to Optima Voice, where your AI agent will pick them up.

## Other Providers

If your provider is not listed above, look for one of these options in your account settings:

| What to look for | What to enter |
|-----------------|---------------|
| SIP destination / SIP URI | `sip:sip.optima-voice.com:5060` |
| Termination URI | `sip:sip.optima-voice.com:5060` |
| Call forwarding destination | `sip:sip.optima-voice.com:5060` |
| Trunk destination | `sip:sip.optima-voice.com:5060` |
| SIP server / SIP host | `sip.optima-voice.com` (port `5060`) |

If you're unsure, search your provider's help center for "forward calls to a SIP URI" or contact their support.

---

## Step 3: Test It

Once you've saved the settings in your provider:

1. Call your phone number from another phone
2. Your Optima Voice agent should answer within a few seconds
3. Check **Call History** in Optima Voice to confirm the call was received

<Tip>
It can take up to 2 minutes for your provider's settings to take effect. If the call doesn't connect right away, wait a moment and try again.
</Tip>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Calls aren't reaching my agent">
    **Check these first:**
    - Did you save the new destination in your provider's settings?
    - Is the SIP destination entered exactly as shown? (`sip:sip.optima-voice.com:5060`)
    - Is your phone number added in Optima Voice with the correct format? (must start with + and country code)
    - Is an agent assigned to the number in Optima Voice?

    If everything looks right, wait 2 minutes and try again — provider settings sometimes take a moment to propagate.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Calls connect but there's no audio">
    This is usually a setting on your provider's side. Try:
    - Making sure your provider allows calls to external SIP destinations
    - Checking that your provider account is active and in good standing
    - Contacting your provider's support and asking them to verify routing to `sip.optima-voice.com:5060`
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I can't find where to set the SIP destination in my provider">
    Look for terms like:
    - "Call forwarding"
    - "SIP trunking"
    - "Termination settings"
    - "Inbound routing"
    - "Voice settings"

    If you still can't find it, contact your provider's support team and say: _"I need to forward inbound calls to an external SIP address: sip:sip.optima-voice.com:5060"_
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

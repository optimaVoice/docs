Create an AI voice agent that answers calls, books appointments, and handles customer questions.

## What you'll build

1. Create your account
2. Build your first agent
3. Choose a voice
4. Add a phone number
5. Make a test call

**Time required:** 5 minutes

---

## Step 1: Create your account

1. Go to [app.optima-voice.com](https://app.optima-voice.com)
2. Click **Sign Up**
3. Choose: Email, Google, or GitHub
4. Verify your email (if using email)

<Tip>
Google or GitHub sign-up is fastest — no email verification needed.
</Tip>

---

## Step 2: Create your first agent

1. Click **Create Agent** on your dashboard
2. Enter your **Business Name**
3. Write a short **Business Description** (2-3 sentences)
   - Example: "We're a dental office offering cleanings, fillings, and whitening. Open Mon-Fri 9AM-5PM."
4. Describe what the agent should **handle** (answer questions, book appointments, etc.)
5. Click **Generate Agent**

Our AI creates the greeting, instructions, and personality automatically.

<Note>
The more details you provide, the better your agent performs. Include hours, services, and policies.
</Note>

---

## Step 3: Choose a voice

1. On the agent page, find **Voice**
2. Browse or search for voices
3. Click **play** to preview any voice
4. Click to select your choice

<Tip>
Match the voice to your business. A law firm sounds professional with a calm voice. A retail store works well with something friendly and energetic.
</Tip>

---

## Step 4: Add a phone number

### Option A: Purchase a number (Recommended)

1. Go to **Phone Numbers**
2. Click **Add Number**
3. Select your **Country**
4. Browse available numbers
5. Click **Purchase** ($1-5/month)

### Option B: Use your existing number

1. Click **Add Number**
2. Select **Manual Entry**
3. Enter your current phone number
4. We'll give you forwarding instructions

---

## Step 5: Assign the number to your agent

1. On the Phone Numbers page, find your number
2. Click **Assign to Agent**
3. Select your agent
4. Click **Assign**

Your agent is now live.

---

## Step 6: Make a test call

1. Click **Test** on your agent page
2. Call the number from your phone (or use in-browser)
3. Listen for:
   - Clear greeting
   - Correct answers
   - Good voice quality

<Tip>
First calls take 2-3 seconds to connect. The agent is initializing.
</Tip>

---

## Adjust if needed

Not satisfied? Change these anytime:

- **Voice** — Switch to a different voice
- **Instructions** — Edit what your agent knows
- **Greeting** — Update the opening message

## Plans at a glance

Optima Voice uses **per-call billing** — you pay based on how many calls your agents handle, not how long each call lasts. Simple, predictable pricing.

| Plan | Price | Calls/month | Agents | Phone numbers |
|------|-------|-------------|--------|---------------|
| **Free Trial** | $0 | 10 calls | 1 | Widget only |
| **Starter** | $49/mo | 75 calls | 1 | 1 included |
| **Growth** | $149/mo | 250 calls | 3 | 2 included |
| **Business** | $349/mo | 750 calls | 10 | 5 included |

<Tip>
All paid plans include: call recordings, transcripts, knowledge base, and actions. Need more phone numbers? Add extras for $3/month each.
</Tip>

## Overage calls

If your agents handle more calls than your plan includes, additional calls are billed automatically:

| Plan | Overage rate |
|------|-------------|
| Starter | $0.75/call |
| Growth | $0.70/call |
| Business | $0.55/call |

<Tip>
Monitor your usage in **Settings → Billing** to track how many calls you've used this cycle.
</Tip>

## Test calls

Test calls made from the in-browser widget on your agent's page are **free on all plans** and do not count toward your monthly call limit. You can make up to 50 test calls per day.

This lets you refine your agent's behavior without worrying about usage.

<Note>
Only calls made via the **Test** button in the dashboard count as test calls. Calls from real phone numbers (inbound calls to your purchased number) count toward your plan.
</Note>

## Free trial details

Every new account starts with a **14-day free trial** that includes 10 calls:

- No credit card required to start
- Build and configure your agent immediately
- Test via the in-browser widget
- When you're running low on calls, you'll see a banner in your dashboard
- We'll also send you an email heads-up before your trial calls run out

When your trial ends (14 days or 10 calls used, whichever comes first), upgrade to a paid plan to keep your agents live.

<Note>
Your agents, knowledge base, and settings are preserved after the trial ends. Nothing gets deleted — just upgrade to pick up where you left off.
</Note>

## Upgrading

1. Go to **Settings → Billing**
2. Click **Upgrade Plan**
3. Select your new plan
4. Enter payment info

<Note>
Upgrades take effect immediately. You'll be charged the prorated difference.
</Note>

---

## Downgrading

1. Go to **Settings → Billing**
2. Click **Change Plan**
3. Select the lower plan
4. Confirm

<Warning>
Downgrades take effect at the end of your billing cycle. You'll keep current features until then. If your new plan has fewer agents or phone numbers than you currently use, you'll need to remove the extras before the downgrade takes effect.
</Warning>

## Cancellation

1. Go to **Settings → Billing**
2. Click **Cancel Subscription**
3. Confirm

<Note>
You keep access until the end of your billing period. No refunds for partial months.
</Note>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What counts as a call?">
    Every inbound call to your phone number counts as one call, regardless of duration. Test calls from the dashboard widget do not count.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens when I run out of calls?">
    If you have overage billing enabled, additional calls are charged at your plan's overage rate. Otherwise, your agent stops taking calls until the next billing cycle. You can upgrade anytime for more calls.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change plans anytime?">
    Yes. Upgrades are immediate. Downgrades take effect at billing cycle end.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do unused calls roll over?">
    No. Each billing period starts fresh.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a refund policy?">
    We don't offer refunds for partial months. Contact support for special cases.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What's the difference between test calls and real calls?">
    Test calls are made from the in-browser widget on your agent's dashboard page — they're free (up to 50/day) and don't count toward your plan. Real calls come in through your purchased phone number and count toward your monthly limit.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Optima Voice integrates with the tools you already use so your AI agent can take action during and after calls.

---

## Available integrations

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Google Calendar" icon="calendar">
    Book, reschedule, and check appointments in real-time during calls. Your agent sees availability and confirms bookings instantly.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Email (SMTP)" icon="envelope">
    Send booking confirmations, follow-up summaries, and custom emails automatically after calls.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Webhooks" icon="webhook">
    Send real-time call data to any system — your CRM, database, Zapier, Make, or custom backend.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Phone Forwarding" icon="phone-arrow-right">
    Transfer calls to a human when your agent can't handle the request. Route to different numbers based on the situation.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

---

## How integrations work

Integrations in Optima Voice are configured as **Actions** — tasks your agent can perform during or after a call.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to your agent's Actions tab">
    Open your agent in the dashboard and click **Actions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an action">
    Choose the integration type (Calendar, Email, Webhook, etc.) and configure the connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define the trigger">
    Tell your agent when to use this action — for example, "when a caller wants to book an appointment."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    Make a test call and verify the integration fires correctly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
See the [Actions & Integrations](/actions) page for detailed setup instructions for each integration type.
</Tip>

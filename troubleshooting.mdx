## Can't connect or call not answered

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Agent doesn't answer when I call">
    **Check these things:**

    1. Is the number assigned to an agent?
       - Go to Phone Numbers → find your number
       - Make sure it shows "Active" status

    2. Is the agent configured?
       - Check that Voice is selected
       - Verify Voice Instructions are not empty

    3. Wait 2 minutes
       - Sometimes provisioning takes a minute

    <Tip>
    Try calling from a different phone to rule out your device.
    </Tip>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Call immediately">
    - Check fails your internet connection
    - Refresh the dashboard
    - Try a different network
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="First call takes too long to connect">
    Normal! First call after inactivity takes 2-3 seconds to initialize. Subsequent calls connect faster.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Audio and voice issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Audio quality is poor or choppy">
    **Try these fixes:**

    1. Check your internet speed
       - Need: At least 1 Mbps
       - Test at speedtest.net

    2. Use a different network
       - Switch from WiFi to ethernet
       - Or try a different WiFi

    3. Close other bandwidth-heavy apps
       - Video calls, large downloads

    <Tip>
    If callers report static, ask them to check their connection.
    </Tip>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Voice sounds robotic">
    - Lower the Creativity (Temperature) setting
    - Try a different voice
    - Check: Are responses very short? The AI may be rushing
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Words are mispronounced">
    Add pronunciation hints to Voice Instructions:

    ```
    Pronunciation guide:
    - "Optima" = "Op-tih-mah"
    - "Nguyen" = "Win"
    - "GIF" = "Jif" (not "Gif")
    ```
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Agent talks too fast or too slow">
    Adjust the Speed setting in Voice options. Start around 0.9-1.0 for natural pacing.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Agent behavior problems

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Agent gives wrong or inaccurate information">
    **Fix it:**

    1. Update Voice Instructions with correct info
    2. Upload documents to Knowledge Base
    3. Include specific examples in instructions

    <Tip>
    Instead of "We offer cleaning," write: "Teeth cleaning costs $120 and takes 45 minutes."
    </Tip>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Agent is too brief or doesn't elaborate">
    Add to Voice Instructions:
    ```
    - Provide thorough answers
    - Include relevant details
    - Ask if they need more information
    ```
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Agent is too chatty">
    Add to Voice Instructions:
    ```
    - Keep responses concise
    - Under 30 seconds unless caller asks for more
    - Get to the point quickly
    ```
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Agent doesn't use the knowledge base">
    Make sure Voice Instructions mention it:
    ```
    Use the Knowledge Base to answer detailed questions about our services, pricing, and policies.
    ```
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Agent hangs up unexpectedly">
    Check Max Call Duration in Advanced Settings. Increase it if calls are ending too soon.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Action and integration issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Calendar bookings aren't working">
    1. Reconnect Google Calendar
    2. Check that Calendar has availability
    3. Verify permissions in Google account
    4. Make sure instructions mention the calendar
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Emails aren't sending">
    1. Reconnect Gmail
    2. Check: Is the customer email valid?
    3. Verify "Send Email" action is enabled
    for errors 4. Look in Call Management
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Slack notifications not working">
    1. Reconnect Slack
    2. Check: Is the channel name correct?
    3. Verify action is enabled in agent settings
    4. Make sure bot has channel access
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Account and billing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can't access the dashboard">
    - Clear browser cache
    - Try incognito/private mode
    - Use a different browser
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Payment won't go through">
    - Check: Card number, expiry, CVV correct?
    - Try a different card
    - Contact your bank
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Calls used up">
    - Upgrade your plan in **Settings → Billing** for more included calls
    - Additional calls beyond your plan are billed at your overage rate
    - Test calls from the dashboard widget are always free (up to 50/day)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Trial expired">
    - Your 14-day trial includes 10 calls
    - Upgrade to any paid plan to continue — your agents and settings are preserved
    - Go to **Settings → Billing → Upgrade Plan**
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Call recordings and transcripts

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Recording doesn't appear">
    Wait 30 seconds after call ends, then refresh. Some calls (very short or failed) may not have recordings.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Transcript is inaccurate">
    Poor audio causes bad transcripts. Improve audio quality and try again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can't find a specific call">
    - Clear all filters
    - Check the date range
    - Note: test calls from the dashboard widget appear separately from real inbound calls
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

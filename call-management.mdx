Every call is recorded and transcribed. Review conversations to ensure quality and improve your agent.

---

## Viewing calls

1. Click **Calls** in the sidebar
2. See your call history

Each row shows:
- Agent name
- Date and time
- Duration
- Status (Completed, Failed, In Progress)

### Filter calls

Use filters to find specific calls:
- By agent
- By status (Completed, Failed)
- By date (Today, This Week, This Month)

---

## Call details

Click any call to see:

### Overview
- Start time
- Duration
- Status
- Agent name
- Phone number

### Recording

Click **Play** to listen. Use controls to:
- Play/Pause
- Seek
- Change speed (1x, 1.5x, 2x)
- Download as MP3

### Transcript

Read the full conversation. Each line shows:
- Who spoke (Agent or Customer)
- What was said
- Timestamps

<Tip>
Use transcripts to find common questions. If customers ask the same thing repeatedly, add it to your knowledge base or agent instructions.
</Tip>

---

## Call statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Completed | Call ended normally |
| Failed | Connection error or timeout |
| In Progress | Call is happening now |

---

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Recording missing">
    Wait 30 seconds after the call ends, then refresh. Failed calls may not have recordings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Transcript unclear">
    Poor audio quality affects transcription. Check the recording. Enable noise cancellation in phone number settings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can't find a call">
    Clear filters. Check the date range. Calls are kept for 90 days.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

---

## Data retention

| Plan | Recording storage |
|------|-------------------|
| Free, Lite, Starter | 90 days |
| Pro, Business | 180 days |

Download recordings before they expire.

---

## Privacy

- Recordings are encrypted
- Only your team can access
- Customers can request opt-out where required

<Note>
Update your greeting to: "This call may be recorded for quality assurance."
</Tip>

## Data encryption

All data is encrypted at rest and in transit:

- **In transit** — TLS 1.2+ for all API and web traffic, SRTP for voice calls
- **At rest** — AES-256 encryption for stored recordings, transcripts, and documents

---

## Infrastructure

Optima Voice runs on enterprise-grade cloud infrastructure with:

- SOC 2 Type II certified hosting providers
- Automated backups with geographic redundancy
- 99.9% uptime SLA for paid plans
- DDoS protection and Web Application Firewall (WAF)

## Voice & call data

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Call recordings">
    Recordings are stored encrypted and accessible only to your organization. Storage duration depends on your plan (90 days to 1 year). You can delete recordings at any time from your dashboard.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Transcripts">
    Call transcripts are generated in real-time and stored with the same encryption and retention policies as recordings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Knowledge base documents">
    Documents you upload are processed and stored securely. They are never shared across organizations or used to train AI models.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## GDPR compliance

Optima Voice supports GDPR compliance:

- **Data access** — Export all your organization's data at any time
- **Data deletion** — Request complete data deletion via support
- **Data portability** — Download recordings, transcripts, and configurations
- **Consent** — Configure your agent to announce call recording when required by local law

---

## Access control

- Email-based authentication with secure password requirements
- Session management with automatic timeouts
- Organization-level data isolation — your data is never accessible to other accounts

---

## Data retention

| Plan | Recording retention |

| Free | 90 days |
| Lite | 90 days |
| Starter | 90 days |
| Pro | 180 days |
| Business | 1 year |

You can delete any recording or transcript manually at any time.

## Responsible AI

- Your knowledge base documents are used only for your agents — never for model training
- Call data stays within your organization
- AI responses are grounded in your uploaded knowledge to minimize hallucination

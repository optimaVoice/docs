## New pricing: per-call billing

We've moved from per-minute to **per-call billing**. Every inbound call counts as one call, regardless of duration — simpler and more predictable.

**New plans:**

| Plan | Price | Calls/month | Agents | Phone numbers |
|------|-------|-------------|--------|---------------|
| Free Trial | $0 | 10 calls | 1 | Widget only |
| Starter | $49/mo | 75 calls | 1 | 1 included |
| Growth | $149/mo | 250 calls | 3 | 2 included |
| Business | $349/mo | 750 calls | 10 | 5 included |

Need more phone numbers? Add extras for **$3/month each**. Annual billing saves **15%**.

See full details on the [Billing](/billing) page.

---

## Free test calls

Test calls from the in-browser widget are now **completely free** on all plans — up to 50 per day. They don't count toward your monthly call limit, so you can tune your agent's behavior without worrying about usage.

---

## Improved trial experience

The free trial now includes **10 calls over 14 days**. We've also added:

- A dashboard banner when you're running low on trial calls
- Email notifications before your trial calls run out
- Smoother upgrade flow — your agents, knowledge base, and settings are all preserved when you upgrade

---

## US phone numbers

Phone number purchasing is now focused on **US numbers** (local and toll-free). If you need international numbers, you can connect them via [SIP trunking](/sip-trunks/manual-setup) with your own provider.

---

## Better error messages

All user-facing error messages are now human-readable and translated into all 9 supported languages. No more technical jargon or JSON in error toasts.

---

## Mobile improvements

The dashboard is now fully responsive across all pages — better layouts, navigation, and readability on phones and tablets.

---

## Dark mode fixes

Fixed color inconsistencies in dark mode across the dashboard. All UI elements now use proper theme tokens.

---

## Stability improvements

- Improved call connection reliability
- Fixed sign-in redirect issues that could cause login loops
- Better handling of billing edge cases during plan transitions

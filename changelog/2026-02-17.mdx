## Annual billing

You can now switch between monthly and annual billing directly from your dashboard. Annual plans save you **22%** — that's nearly 3 months free.

Toggle between billing periods in **Settings → Billing** at any time.

---

## Multi-language support

Your agents can now speak **9 languages**:

- English
- Spanish
- French
- German
- Portuguese
- Italian
- Dutch
- Polish
- Russian

Select your agent's language in **Agent Settings → Language**. Each language includes a curated selection of native-sounding voices.

---

## New Lite plan

We've added a **Lite plan at $95/month** for small teams that need a professional AI receptionist without the full Starter plan. Includes 150 minutes, unlimited agents, and 3 phone numbers.

See all plans on the [Billing](/billing) page.

---

## Improved onboarding

The agent creation flow is now faster and more intuitive. We've streamlined the setup wizard so most users can go from signup to live agent in under 5 minutes.

---

## Enhanced call recordings & transcripts

Call recordings and transcripts now display in an improved player with:

- Synchronized transcript highlighting
- Quick-jump to any part of the conversation
- Download options for both audio and text
- Better mobile experience

Find your recordings in **Calls → Call History**.

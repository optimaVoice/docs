---

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="How long does setup take?">
    Most users are live in under 5 minutes. Create an account, build your agent, choose a voice, add a phone number — done.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need technical skills?">
    No. Optima Voice is designed for business owners, not developers. If you can use a website, you can set up an AI call agent.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does the AI know about my business?">
    You upload documents (menus, price lists, FAQs, service descriptions) to the [Knowledge Base](/knowledge-base). Your agent uses this information to answer caller questions accurately.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can the agent book appointments?">
    Yes. Connect your Google Calendar and your agent will check availability, book appointments, and send email confirmations — all during the call. See [Actions](/actions) for setup.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if the AI can't handle a question?">
    You can configure your agent to transfer the call to a human when it encounters something outside its knowledge. Set this up in your agent's [Actions](/actions).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use my existing phone number?">
    Yes. Either forward your current number to your Optima Voice number, or connect it directly via SIP trunking with [Telnyx](/sip-trunks/telnyx-setup), [Twilio](/sip-trunks/twilio-setup), or [Zadarma](/sip-trunks/zadarma-setup).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What languages are supported?">
    Optima Voice supports 9 languages: English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Polish, and Russian.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are calls recorded?">
    Yes. All calls are recorded and transcribed automatically. You can review them in your dashboard under **Calls → Call History**. Recordings are stored based on your plan's retention period.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change plans anytime?">
    Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades apply at the end of your billing cycle. See [Billing](/billing).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when I run out of minutes?">
    Your agent stops taking calls until the next billing cycle, or you can upgrade to a higher plan for more minutes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a free plan?">
    Yes. The Free plan gives you 20 minutes of calls with 1 agent and 1 phone number — no credit card required. Perfect for testing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do unused minutes roll over?">
    No. Each billing period starts fresh. Monitor your usage in **Settings → Usage**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I cancel?">
    Go to **Settings → Billing → Cancel Subscription**. You keep access until the end of your billing period.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is my data secure?">
    Yes. All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+). Voice calls use SRTP encryption. See our [Security](/security) page for details.
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

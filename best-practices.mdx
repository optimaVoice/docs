## Writing great agent instructions

### Start with the basics

Your instructions tell your agent who it is and what it knows. The better your instructions, the better your agent performs.

**Include:**

- Business name and type
- Hours of operation
- Services or products offered
- Pricing (if public)
- Common questions and answers
- How to handle bookings

### Be specific

**Bad:** "Answer questions about our services."

**Good:** "We offer three services: house cleaning ($120), deep cleaning ($200), and move-out cleaning ($250). House cleaning takes 2-3 hours. Deep cleaning takes 4-5 hours."

<Tip>
The AI isn't guessing. Tell it exactly what to say.
</Tip>

### Set boundaries

Tell your agent what NOT to do:

```
Do not:
- Discuss competitor pricing
- Give medical advice (transfer to doctor)
- Promise specific outcomes
- Discuss pricing with anyone under 18
```

### Use examples

Show, don't just tell:

```
Example responses:

Question: "How much is a cleaning?"
Response: "Our standard house cleaning is $120, which includes up to 2,000 square feet. That covers dusting, vacuuming, mopping, and bathroom cleaning."

Question: "Are you open on Sundays?"
Response: "We're closed on Sundays. Our regular hours are Monday through Saturday, 9AM to 5PM."
```

## Choosing the right voice

### Match your business

| Business type | Voice style |
|---------------|-------------|
| Law firm, accountant | Professional, authoritative |
| Medical, health | Calm, reassuring |
| Restaurant, retail | Friendly, energetic |
| Technical support | Clear, patient |
| Real estate | Warm, professional |

### Preview before deciding

1. Listen to 3-5 voices
2. Test with your actual greeting
3. Ask: "Does this sound like I'd want this person answering my phone?"

<Tip>
The default voice isn't always the best. Browse the full library — there are great options throughout.
</Tip>

### Consider your callers

- **B2B businesses:** Slightly formal voices build trust
- **Consumer-facing:** Friendly and approachable works best
- **Professional services:** Authoritative but not cold

## Optimizing call quality

### For your connection

- Use stable internet (not mobile data)
- Close unused apps and tabs
- Use headphones if possible

### For caller experience

- Keep greetings under 15 seconds
- Have agent confirm important info ("So that's 2pm on Thursday?")
- End calls by summarizing next steps

### For recording quality

- Avoid speakerphone when possible
- Minimize background noise
- Speak clearly and at normal pace

## Using the knowledge base

### What to upload

- Price lists and menus
- Service descriptions
- FAQs
- Policies (returns, cancellation, etc.)
- Company history or About Us

### How to format

**DO:**
- Use plain text or PDF
- Write in complete sentences
- Use headings to organize sections

**DON'T:**
- Upload scanned images
- Use complex tables
- Include handwritten notes

### Keep it current

- Review knowledge base monthly
- Update prices when they change
- Remove outdated policies

<Tip>
Add a "Last updated" note at the top of documents so you know what's changed.
</Tip>

## Getting better over time

### Review calls regularly

- Listen to 2-3 calls per week
- Note common questions
- Update instructions based on what you hear

### Track patterns

Use Call Management to find:
- Questions asked repeatedly → add to knowledge base
- Confusion points → clarify in instructions
- Successful interactions → use as examples

### Test often

- Call your agent yourself
- Ask tricky questions
- Pretend you're a real customer

<Tip>
Set a weekly reminder to review calls. It takes 15 minutes and dramatically improves performance.
</Tip>

---

## Common mistakes to avoid

### 1. Being too vague

The AI doesn't know your business unless you tell it. Don't assume it knows common sense stuff.

### 2. Forgetting to update

Business changes but agent instructions stay the same. Set calendar reminders to review monthly.

### 3. Ignoring caller feedback

If callers seem confused, your instructions need work. Listen and improve.

### 4. Setting and forgetting

The best agents improve over time. Regular updates keep your agent sharp.

Before launching your agent:

- [ ] Business name and hours in instructions
- [ ] Services and pricing included

- [ ] Tested with real calls
- [ ] Greeting under 15 seconds
- [ ] Max call duration set appropriately

Print this checklist. Use it every time you make major changes.
